ABOUT

A short profile without the artist-statement fog.

I’m M/:T, a RUS / ENG glitchcore and visual designer. I’ve been doing this for years.

TouchDesigner is the tool I return to most, but I don’t care much about staying inside one program. If something gives me the right texture, movement or damage, I use it.

My work is tied to sedation. Making things gives psychological pressure somewhere to go, and I don’t clean that part up for presentation; it ends up in the image.
