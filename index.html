PROFILE / M/:T

I AM A RUS / ENG GLITCHCORE & VISUAL DESIGNER WORKING WITH TOUCHDESIGNER AND OTHER EXPERIMENTAL VISUAL TOOLS.

I have been inside this field for years, building a language around broken images, procedural systems, 3D forms and controlled visual instability.

My practice is closely tied to sedation through creation: psychological pressure is translated into color, texture, distortion and rhythm instead of being hidden. The result is personal, imperfect and deliberately alive.
