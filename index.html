ABOUT

I’m M/:T. Visual designer, glitchcore designer and psychological artist.

TouchDesigner is the tool I return to most. I work with images, motion and visual systems, then switch tools when I need a different kind of texture or damage.

I put psychological problems into the work because that is where they end up anyway. I like an avant-garde edge. I don’t try to make the pressure look cleaner than it is.
