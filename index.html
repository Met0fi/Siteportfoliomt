ABOUT

I’m M/:T. I work with glitch, damaged images and 3D material, mostly in TouchDesigner.

I use the work to put psychological pressure somewhere visible. I keep breaks, artifacts and rough edges when they carry that pressure instead of cleaning them up for polish.

TouchDesigner is where most pieces begin. I switch to Glitch Lab or other tools when the image needs a different kind of texture, distortion or spatial treatment.
